SnapUI

Screenshots that go straight to Claude. Free, for macOS 15 or later.

Install (recommended)

Open Terminal (in Applications → Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://sprouthouse.github.io/snapui-updates/install.sh | bash

It downloads the latest SnapUI, checks it, puts it in Applications and opens it. No security warning, and SnapUI keeps itself up to date after that.

Or download the disk image

SnapUI.dmg. Drag SnapUI into Applications. macOS will say it can't check it for malware, because SnapUI isn't sold through Apple:

  1. Click Done on that message.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to SnapUI, then confirm.

Everything SnapUI captures stays on your Mac until you send it.
